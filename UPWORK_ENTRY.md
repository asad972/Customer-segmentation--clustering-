# Upwork portfolio entry

**Project title**
Customer Segmentation & RFM Analysis for an Online Retailer

**Your role**
Data Scientist - personal project

**Project description**
Segmented 5,852 customers of a UK online retailer from two years of real transactions (1M+ rows, £17.1M revenue), with RFM features and K-means clustering. I cleaned and validated the data, chose 5 segments via the elbow and silhouette methods, and benchmarked it against a Gaussian Mixture model. Key finding: "Champions" are 18% of customers but drive 74% of revenue. 1,172 lapsed customers had larger average orders than loyal ones (£416 vs £306), which makes them a clear win-back target. Each segment has a concrete marketing action, backed by clear charts and a reproducible Python pipeline.
